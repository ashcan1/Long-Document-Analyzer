Quarterly Financial Report - Q3 2026

Executive Summary

The company delivered strong performance in the third quarter of 2026, with revenue increasing 12% year-over-year to $4.2 billion. Operating income rose 18% to $980 million, driven by improved cost efficiency and strong demand across all business segments.

Revenue Breakdown

The Technology segment contributed $1.8 billion, representing 43% of total revenue, up from 38% in the prior year quarter. The Services segment generated $1.4 billion, a 9% increase year-over-year. The Hardware segment produced $1.0 billion, roughly flat compared to the same period last year.

Operating Expenses

Total operating expenses were $3.22 billion, compared to $2.98 billion in Q3 2025. Research and development spending increased 22% to $420 million as the company accelerated investment in artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities. Sales and marketing expenses were $680 million, up 8% year-over-year. General and administrative costs were held flat at $210 million.

Cash Flow and Balance Sheet

The company generated $1.1 billion in free cash flow during the quarter, bringing the year-to-date total to $3.0 billion. Cash and equivalents on the balance sheet stood at $8.4 billion at the end of the quarter. The company repurchased $500 million of its own shares during the period and paid $120 million in dividends.

Outlook

For Q4 2026, the company expects revenue in the range of $4.4 billion to $4.6 billion, representing growth of 10% to 15% year-over-year. Operating margin is expected to be between 22% and 24%. The full-year 2026 revenue guidance is raised to $16.5 billion to $16.8 billion.
